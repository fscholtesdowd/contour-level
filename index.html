How to use it

  1. Mount or hold the phone flat against your A-frame crossbar or bunyip board.
  2. Set the tool down on ground you know is level (or use a bubble level to true it up), then tap Start and allow motion access.
  3. Tap Calibrate once — this cancels out any mounting angle, so the phone doesn't have to sit perfectly flat.
  4. Walk the hill, pivoting the tool leg to leg. A steady tone means level. The beep speeds up and the pitch rises when one leg is high.
  5. Switch to Slope offset and drag the slider if you want a gentle fall (1–2%) for a drainage ditch instead of dead-level for a swale.
  6. Tap + Mark stake each time you drive a marking stake, just to keep count as you walk.

Works best in a browser tab, screen on, phone mounted to the tool. On iPhone, Safari will ask for a one-time motion & orientation permission — tap Allow. Keep the screen awake in your phone's settings while you work; this page can't do that for you.
